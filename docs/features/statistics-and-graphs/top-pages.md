# Top Pages

Top Pages lists the most visited pages of your site.

By default, UTM parameters are removed from the saved URL, so campaign links to one page are counted as the same page. See [Tracking UTM parameters](../../guides/tracking-utm-parameters.md).

It is shown in the [statistics view](README.md) with [Top Countries](top-countries.md) and [Top Referrers](top-referrers.md).
